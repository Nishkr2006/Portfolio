## Featured Projects

- **Coven – Accessibility-Focused Survival Planner** *(Hack or Treat – UofT Hacks)*  
  🏆 **Best Standard Hack – Winner**  
  A student survival planner with deadline tracking, "Crunch Moon" workload visualizations, and balanced study session scheduling built with React, TypeScript, Vite, and Zustand.

- **SentryPay – Credit Card Fraud Analysis**  
  Analyzed ~1.3 million credit card transactions in Python to investigate financial fraud patterns in highly imbalanced data (0.58% fraud rate) and audited timestamp anomalies.

- **Lung Cancer Prediction – Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning**  
  Comparative study of 6 models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, MLP, TabNet, FT-Transformer) on 5,000 patient records, focusing on reducing false negatives in healthcare.

- **Blue Jays Pitching Analytics**  
  Investigated MLB Statcast pitching patterns using Python and scikit-learn (ROC-AUC 0.692) alongside an interactive Power BI dashboard.

- **Nutella Dark – Market Entry & Financial Strategy**  
  Simulated consumer packaged goods business case with scenario financial modelling, break-even analysis (111,359 units), and market sensitivity testing.

## Design & Architecture

- **Visual Vibe:** Mission Control aesthetic customized with a deep obsidian black (`#07050b`) and dark purple (`#100b1a`) palette.
- **Hero Visualization:** Dynamic multi-layer Neural Network SVG with interactive signal pulse packets traversing synaptic layers ($4 \to 5 \to 5 \to 3$ nodes) and accessible pause/play controls.
- **Typography:** Michroma for console display headings, Manrope for body text, and Martian Mono for telemetry labels.
- **Accessibility:** Pure vanilla semantic HTML5, responsive CSS, high contrast (WCAG AAA compliant), and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
